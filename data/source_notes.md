# Source attribution and coverage

## Punjab

- **Title:** B-28: “Other workers” by distance from residence to place of work and mode of travel to place of work, Punjab — 2011.
- **Producer:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India.
- **Catalog:** https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/13957
- **Download:** https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/13957/download/17070/DDW-0300B-28.xlsx
- **Local file:** `data/raw/DDW-0300B-28.xlsx`
- **Unit/universe:** aggregate persons; other workers.
- **Coverage used:** district aggregates for Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Amritsar, Patiala, and Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar (displayed as Mohali district); Total, Urban and Rural.

## Chandigarh

- **Title:** B-28: “Other workers” by distance from residence to place of work and mode of travel to place of work, Chandigarh — 2011.
- **Producer:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India.
- **Catalog:** https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/13958
- **Download:** https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/13958/download/17071/DDW-0400B-28.xlsx
- **Local file:** `data/raw/DDW-0400B-28.xlsx`
- **Unit/universe:** aggregate persons; other workers.
- **Coverage used:** Chandigarh district/UT aggregate; Total, Urban and Rural.

Both are 2011 historical census tables. They describe commute mode and distance, not individual trips or app bookings. City names may correspond to a district-level aggregate, not the municipal boundary. The source files are retained unchanged; normalization outputs are generated separately.
